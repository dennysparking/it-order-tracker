# Receiving Orders — Guide for Tami, Desiree & Stacey

This is a quick guide for the two things you'll do in the IT Order Tracker:

1. **Confirm** an order once you've placed it (from the email you receive).
2. **Mark items as delivered** when they arrive — and add a note or photo.

You do **not** need a login or password for any of this. Everything works on a computer or your phone.

> The web address for the tracker will be given to you by IT (Rob or Dennis). Wherever this guide shows `http://SERVER`, use the address they give you.

---

## Part 1 — Confirming an order you've placed

When IT sends you an order request, you'll get an **email** listing the item (or items) to order. After you actually place the order with the vendor:

1. Open that email.
2. Scroll to the bottom — you'll see a line like:

   > *Please click the link below to confirm this item has been ordered:*
   > `http://SERVER/confirm/abc123...`

3. **Click the link.** A page opens that says **"Order Confirmed! 🎉"**
4. That's it — the tracker automatically updates the order to **"Ordered."** You don't need to do anything else.

**Good to know**
- If you click the link a second time, it just says **"Already Confirmed."** That's normal — no harm done.
- For a **bulk order** (several items in one request), the single link confirms the whole order at once.
- Only click it **after** you've actually placed the order, since it tells IT the order is on its way.

---

## Part 2 — Your orders page

Each of you has your own page that shows just the orders assigned to you.

- Go to: **`http://SERVER/recipient/YourName`**
  - Tami → `http://SERVER/recipient/Tami`
  - Desiree → `http://SERVER/recipient/Desiree`
  - Stacey → `http://SERVER/recipient/Stacey`
- **Bookmark this page** (or "Add to Home Screen" on your phone) so it's one tap away.

The page shows:
- **Outstanding** — items still on the way.
- **Delivered** — items you've already marked as received.
- A count of each at the top.

It works the same on a computer or a phone.

---

## Part 3 — Marking an item as delivered

When a package arrives:

1. Open your orders page (Part 2).
2. Find the item under **Outstanding**.
3. Tap **"Mark as Delivered."**
4. A box pops up where you can add (both optional, but helpful):
   - **Delivery Notes** — e.g. *"Left at front desk,"* *"Given to Jane in Accounting,"* *"In the IT closet."*
   - **Photo** — tap **"📷 Take or choose a photo."** On a phone this opens your camera so you can snap the package or where you left it.
5. Tap **"Confirm Delivered."**

What happens next:
- The **IT team is automatically notified** by email and in the app.
- Your note and photo are saved with the order so IT can see exactly what happened.
- The item moves from **Outstanding** to **Delivered** on your page.

You don't have to add a note or photo — but a quick note about *where* the item went saves IT a lot of follow-up questions.

## Questions & fixes

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| An order isn't showing on your page | It may not be assigned to you yet — check with IT. |
| You marked the wrong item delivered | Contact IT (Rob or Dennis); they can fix the status. |
| The confirmation link won't open | Make sure you're on the company network, then try again, or let IT know. |
| You're not sure if you already confirmed | Click the link again — it will tell you if it's already confirmed. |

**Need help?** Contact **Rob** or **Dennis** in IT.
